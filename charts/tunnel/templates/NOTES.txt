Two classes were created, one per tunnel provider:

  tunnel.pizza            named hostnames under tunneled.pizza
  api.trycloudflare.com   Cloudflare quick tunnels, no account, no us

Opt a workload in (nothing is claimed implicitly — neither is a default class):

  spec.ingressClassName: tunnel.pizza
  spec.gatewayClassName: tunnel.pizza

A claimed Ingress gets a tunnel to its backend Service; its hostname shows up in
the ADDRESS column of `kubectl get ingress` in that workload's own namespace.
Gateways are still a scaffold and receive an "Unimplemented" event.

Point one at a different control plane with an annotation, on the resource or on
its class:

  tunnel.pizza/provider: my-own-host.example
